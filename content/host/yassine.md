Yassine is in Year 11 at a public school.

"I think it is important for the younger generation to be interested in and aware of policy and its impacts on their current and future lives - especially since a lot of young people have no interest in government policies. 

I joined Why Can't They Just and Policy 4 People because I think that it is important to have the perspective of a younger Gen Z. I think people my age need to be a part of the discussion and taken into consideration when talking about the impacts of policy. Since I have lots of strong opinions about things, I can express them in the context of policy through the podcast."
